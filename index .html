A home for worship and learning

Our New Mosque & Centre

A shared place for daily prayers, the Academy, lectures, conferences and Da'awah activities.

Alhamdulillah! This is our new Mosque & Centre for Aonus-Sunnah Da'awah Centre Nigeria in Angwan Gwari, Suleja, Niger State. The Mosque is for daily prayers, and the Centre is for our Academy, lectures, conferences, and Da'awah activities for Parents & Grownups. May Allah bless everyone who contributed to building this house of Allah.

About the founder

A community built around learning

Sheikh Yusuf Abdussalam Jabrat... founded Aonussunah Islamic Da'wah Group and serves as Chief Missioner of Aonu-Sunnah Islamic Da'wah Academy. He started the Academy in 2017 in Angwan Gwari, Suleja.

Our intention: To propagate Islam, bring Muslims closer to the Qur'an and Sunnah, promote Islamic knowledge, and help Muslims understand their religion correctly.
